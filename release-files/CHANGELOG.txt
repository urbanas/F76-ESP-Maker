ESP Maker - changes
===================

2.2.2
Fixed
- Loot bags: skins that share a model showed only one name, so the other looked missing.
  The entry now lists them all: Guts / Mutated Guts, Gravestone / Drifter,
  Wrapped Present / Holiday "Present", Enclave Case / Fishing Cooler.

2.2.1
Fixed
- Loot bags didn't show their look: when two selected things share a model, the dedicated
  category now wins over the catch-all lists (Junk, Aid & food, CAMP items, Plants (all),
  All outfits, Everything else). The paper bag is also Clean Surgical Tools, the plan blueprint
  is also Fanatics Battle Plans, and so on. Click Build & save again to get it.
Changed
- Rainbow colours are set up the way the game's own animated models do it.

2.2
New look pop-up
- Bigger and resizable (drag its edges). Three presets on top: Glow only, Glow + beam,
  Glow + beam + name - they keep your colour.
- Three parts, one choice each: 1 The item (Outline / Fill / Bright / Off, colour, strength,
  Invisible), 2 Beam / Arrow / Off, 3 Icon / Text / Off. Your saved looks are converted.
- Effects per part: X-ray (seen through walls), Pulse, Rainbow, Spin, Bob, Light runs up (beam)
  and its own Hide after timer.
- Reset button: an item goes back to its category's look, a category to its starting look.
- The ready-made look pictures and the quick bar are gone.
New
- Invisible: the item disappears and only what you add (beam, icon, name) shows. It is still
  there to pick up. Not on enemies.
- Categories "All outfits (ground & display model)" and "All outfits (worn - wearers glow)".
- Foliage (hide it): grass, bushes and trees made invisible - easier to spot things on the ground.
- Edit -> Colour by priority: white rare, gold worth a detour, green useful, red junk.
- Name: "Start with the category" (e.g. "Plans · Tato Planter").
3D preview
- The pop-up's preview is the full 3D view: walk around (WASD), View options for camera, scene,
  light and colours, save a picture.
- Scene: Person, House (now around the item, the item on its floor), Wall behind, Shed - one at
  a time. Junk and compared items go with any of them.
- Adding or removing things no longer moves the camera.
- Faster drawing while you turn it.
- The preview plays the effects: Pulse, Rainbow, Spin, Bob, Light runs up, Hide after.
Changed
- Name sizes are bigger: S / M / L = 1 / 1.5 / 2.5 m. Saved 30 cm names become 1 m.
- A strong outline (60-100 %) also tints the item's face.
- Light mode has stronger colours: picked buttons are solid orange, lines and grey text darker.
Fixed
- The setup saved inside a built mod also held the looks of items you didn't build (colours,
  beam settings...). It now holds only what the mod is built from. Thanks for the report!
- Dark mode: the Cancel button's text was cut in Build & save and other small dialogs.
- The filter buttons above the list sat lower than the buttons beside them.
- The last colour dot was cut on the right when picked.

2.1
New
- Name label: a floating name above the item - the item's own name or a text you type, in three
  sizes. The 3D preview shows it.
- Hide after: the glow, marker, icon and name shrink away after a time you choose (30 s, 1 min,
  2 min or your own). The clock starts when the game loads the item, so it starts again when you
  come back to the area. Not on enemies. Idea from the BobbleBanner mod (no files from it used).
- CAMP items category under Items: 1,569 things players build in their CAMPs.
- Icons next to the categories in the list.
- Search lists the matching categories first; click one (or press Enter) to open it.
- A new 3D icon: Lava block.
3D preview
- The person, house and walls in the bigger view are the game's own models now (a dressed
  mannequin, a Covenant house, CAMP barn walls with a door and windows).
- A person stands beside the item for size. Disturbed graves and other landscape models sit on
  the ground.
- Skull, crown, ghost, leaf, slime and bobblehead 3D icons redrawn.
Changed
- Search: all the words you type must be in one name, and search also finds things in
  Everything else (e.g. CAMP Pumpkin Pie Stand).
- Everything else is always shown; the View -> Show everything else switch is gone.
Fixed
- Less flicker when you click a category.

2.0
New look for the whole app
- A bar on the far left switches between your picks and the sections (items, junk, events,
  enemies...); next to it are that section's categories. Long lists have sub-headings (events by kind,
  junk by material) that fold.
- Looks open in a pop-up: click an item for its own look, click a category for the category's look.
  The pop-up stays open and follows the row you pick, and you can drag it anywhere (it remembers
  where).
- A simpler look editor: ready-made look pictures, a row of colours, and Glow / Marker / Icon /
  Shine cards. An item follows its category's look until you change something.
- Dark mode (View -> Dark mode), also for the dialogs and the 3D view.
- A menu bar (File / Edit / View / Options / Help) instead of the "More" button.
- Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).
- Filters above the list: All / Selected / Own look / Has parts.
- Pick several rows and a bar under the list edits, resets, selects or deselects them all at once.
- Click a column header to sort.
- Ctrl+K jumps to any category or item, and runs actions (Build & save, presets, dark mode...).
- The Look column shows a small picture of each look. "+N shared" marks items that light up
  together with others.
- View -> Compact rows for smaller screens.
3D preview
- "Apply look | Original" switches between your look and the plain game model.
- "Bigger view" opens a large window you can keep open next to the app. It follows the item you
  pick and shows the look settings beside it. In it:
  - drag to turn, WASD / arrow keys to walk, Q / E for height, stand back 10 / 30 / 100 m;
  - Add: a person and floor grid for size, a house, or walls with a doorway to test "Visible
    through walls"; junk and grass around the item; other selected items side by side (up to 12)
    to compare;
  - light: studio, day, dusk, night or fog;
  - colour-blind views (red-green and blue-yellow) to check your colours stand out;
  - spin slowly around the item; fit a tall beam; save the picture as a PNG.
- Plants and landscape models (deathclaw nests) stand on the ground in the preview instead of
  showing their buried half. Shine shows in the preview.
More things to find
- Doors, Apparel on the ground and Moveable objects categories under Everything else
  (View -> Show everything else).
Fixed
- A low glow strength now really is faint: a 5 % outline was almost as bright as 30 %.
  Rebuild your mod to get it.

1.2
New
- Import .ba2: open any ESP mod and change it - your own or someone else's.
  Your own mods made with 1.2 or later come back exactly as you built them. Older ones come back
  with their items and looks (beam length and width start at the category's).
  Other people's mods: the items they change get selected, part by part, and their colours are read
  where possible. Models that aren't in ESP Maker's lists can't be included; the log lists them.
  With something already selected it asks whether to replace it or add to it, so you can merge
  several mods into one. Turn the original mod off in your mod manager once yours is built.
- Build & save: type a name, and the mod is built and installed. Pick one of your mods in the name
  box to replace it. Uncheck "Install in the game" to only make the .ba2.

Changed
- Simpler way of working: the "Mod:" list and the status line are gone. There is one current
  selection; to work on another mod, import it or open its project.
- The bottom of the window lists the ESP Maker mods in the game, read from the mod files themselves.
- Uninstall shows a checklist of your mods, so you pick which to remove.
- Save / Load setup is now Save / Open project.
- The tour shows the new buttons.

Fixes
- Snow globes, Protectron vending machines and some weapons (39 models) were left out of builds and
  shown without the look in the preview. They work now (rebuild your mod to get them).
- An ini that wasn't saved as UTF-8 could get damaged characters when the app updated it.
- If the ini had more than one sResourceArchive2List line, only the first was updated.
- Old 3D-shape looks on the split Spooky Scorched heads and bodies only converted on the second start.

Updating from 1.1 (keeps your setups)
1. Close ESP Maker (and the game).
2. Replace the old "ESP Maker.exe" with the new one. Don't delete %AppData%\ESP Maker.
3. Start the new version. Your installed mods stay in the game. The setups from the old "Mod:" list
   move to %AppData%\ESP Maker\output\my mods (the log says so once).
4. To change a mod: "Import .ba2..." and pick it, or More > "Open project..." and pick its setup in
   "my mods". Then "Build & save" under the same name. Do this once if you want the fixes above.

Heads-up: some things light up more than you asked for
The game highlights 3D MODELS, not items, and many models are reused. Lighting one up lights up
everything that uses the same model - sometimes things in the world you never meant to mark.
- Weapons: guns are built from shared parts (receivers, barrels, stocks...). Checking the 10mm
  Pistol can also light up other guns built on the same parts, and guns on display racks, wall
  mounts or in CAMPs. A dropped weapon with a different barrel or stock only glows on the parts it
  shares with the default version.
- Junk: e.g. the Plastic Gas Canister uses the same model as the raid's fuel canister.
- Events: some event objects are normal world or CAMP objects too (the Most Wanted getaway wagon =
  the CAMP wagon; Eviction Notice meat bags also hang in super mutant camps).
Click an entry and look at Details to see what shares its model. If something glows that you
don't want, uncheck that entry, or open its parts ("► parts") and uncheck the part that causes it.

Updating from 1.0 (keeps your setups)
Your settings, mods and looks are saved in %AppData%\ESP Maker, not in the app folder.
1. Close ESP Maker (and the game).
2. Replace the old "ESP Maker.exe" with the new one (or unzip the new folder anywhere and delete
   the old one). Don't delete %AppData%\ESP Maker.
3. Start the new version: your mods and checks are all there. The first start reads the game's
   item list again (a few seconds).
4. Press "Build & install" once for each of your mods to get the 1.1 fixes into the game.
Your installed .ba2 mods keep working in the game meanwhile - nothing is uninstalled.
