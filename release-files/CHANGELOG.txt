ESP Maker - changes
===================

1.4.1
Fixed
- First aid kits, wall safes, ammo boxes and other animated containers showed no outline glow in
  the game. They now always glow with a fill, whatever look is set. Rebuild your mod to get it.

1.4
New
- Loot bags: dropped-item paper bags, creature meat piles, robot scrap piles and every loot bag skin.
- CAMP storage boxes: aid, ammo, scrap and stash boxes, every design. Every player's box lights up.
  (The "First aid boxes" loot spot is the world's first aid kits, not your CAMP aid box.)
- Deathclaw nests (Scorchbeast nests use the same model and light up too).
- The Pitt expeditions under Events: Union supply crates, chemicals, coolant canisters, deposit
  points, prison doors, trench masks and meds. Each entry says what else lights up.
- Pre-war food: Fancy Lads, Sugar Bombs, Cram, InstaMash, bubblegum and the rest.
- Notes & holotapes: by model (paper, torn paper, holotape, printout...). Notes share a few
  models, so every note on a model lights up together.
- Clothes lying around: clothes and hats on the ground, by their ground model. Clothes whose ground
  model is also the worn one are left out, so players wearing them don't glow.
Fixed
- Chem containers (the Arktos chemical tanks) glowed only on their top and bottom rims. The glass
  tank glows now too.
- Status messages say "selected" instead of "checked".

1.3
New
- Workbenches category: Tinker's, Armor and Weapons Workbenches, Power Armor, Chemistry, Cooking and
  Brewing stations, in the world and in CAMPs.
- Atlantic City expeditions under Events: listening bugs, slot machines, tickets, supplies, secret
  teddy bears and more. Many objectives share their model with other things; each entry says what
  else lights up.
- Bright colours look and Bright plants preset: the item's own colours shine, with nothing added to
  the model and nothing through walls. It works on the game's material, so everything using that
  material brightens too.
Fixed
- A green see-through machine could appear on wood piles when they were highlighted. It's a hidden
  placement guide inside the game's model. These guides are now removed from every highlighted model.
- Spooky Scorched in the Silver Shroud costume get a beam and icon again. It has no head piece, so
  its gloves moved from costume bodies to costume heads.
Changed
- Your picks no longer fills the left panel with a list of counts, so the Categories list keeps its
  room. The counts are in the Categories list itself.
- Guardian (EN06) now lists all 16 of its parts, with clear names, so each one can be switched on or
  off. Before, it had only 4: EN06 is mostly built from normal Sentry Bot parts, so it kept glowing
  after you unselected it.
- Parts EN06 shares with other robots (head, arms, armor, shoulder launchers, laser hands) are marked
  "(shared)" and listed under every robot that uses them: Guardian (EN06), Sentry Bot, Robobrain,
  Protectron and Mr. Handy. Select or unselect a shared part in one place and it changes in all of
  them, because the game uses the same file for all of them.
- Selecting a whole robot selects its shared parts on the others too, without selecting the rest of
  those robots. If you have Sentry Bot selected, EN06's shared parts show as selected. To turn EN06
  off completely, unselect its shared parts: those parts then stop glowing on the other robots too.
- New: the laser hands can now be selected.
- EN06's beam now starts at its torso.

1.2
New
- Import .ba2: open any ESP mod and change it - your own or someone else's.
  Your own mods made with 1.2 or later come back exactly as you built them. Older ones come back
  with their items and looks (beam length and width start at the category's).
  Other people's mods: the items they change get selected, part by part, and their colours are read
  where possible. Models that aren't in ESP Maker's lists can't be included; the log lists them.
  With something already selected it asks whether to replace it or add to it, so you can merge
  several mods into one. Turn the original mod off in your mod manager once yours is built.
- Build & save: type a name, and the mod is built and installed. Pick one of your mods in the name
  box to replace it. Uncheck "Install in the game" to only make the .ba2.

Changed
- Simpler way of working: the "Mod:" list and the status line are gone. There is one current
  selection; to work on another mod, import it or open its project.
- The bottom of the window lists the ESP Maker mods in the game, read from the mod files themselves.
- Uninstall shows a checklist of your mods, so you pick which to remove.
- Save / Load setup is now Save / Open project.
- The tour shows the new buttons.

Fixes
- Snow globes, Protectron vending machines and some weapons (39 models) were left out of builds and
  shown without the look in the preview. They work now (rebuild your mod to get them).
- An ini that wasn't saved as UTF-8 could get damaged characters when the app updated it.
- If the ini had more than one sResourceArchive2List line, only the first was updated.
- Old 3D-shape looks on the split Spooky Scorched heads and bodies only converted on the second start.

Updating from 1.1 (keeps your setups)
1. Close ESP Maker (and the game).
2. Replace the old "ESP Maker.exe" with the new one. Don't delete %AppData%\ESP Maker.
3. Start the new version. Your installed mods stay in the game. The setups from the old "Mod:" list
   move to %AppData%\ESP Maker\output\my mods (the log says so once).
4. To change a mod: "Import .ba2..." and pick it, or More > "Open project..." and pick its setup in
   "my mods". Then "Build & save" under the same name. Do this once if you want the fixes above.

New

Fixes
- Outline glow no longer washes items out white when seen through walls (rebuild your mod to get it).
- Fixed a crash when clicking the list while it was refilling.
- The preview always shows the arrow / icon in the picture.
- Preview and build fall back to the game's own model when another mod's version can't be read.

Heads-up: some things light up more than you asked for
The game highlights 3D MODELS, not items, and many models are reused. Lighting one up lights up
everything that uses the same model - sometimes things in the world you never meant to mark.
- Weapons: guns are built from shared parts (receivers, barrels, stocks...). Checking the 10mm
  Pistol can also light up other guns built on the same parts, and guns on display racks, wall
  mounts or in CAMPs. A dropped weapon with a different barrel or stock only glows on the parts it
  shares with the default version.
- Junk: e.g. the Plastic Gas Canister uses the same model as the raid's fuel canister.
- Events: some event objects are normal world or CAMP objects too (the Most Wanted getaway wagon =
  the CAMP wagon; Eviction Notice meat bags also hang in super mutant camps).
Click an entry and look at Details to see what shares its model. If something glows that you
don't want, uncheck that entry, or open its parts ("► parts") and uncheck the part that causes it.

Updating from 1.0 (keeps your setups)
Your settings, mods and looks are saved in %AppData%\ESP Maker, not in the app folder.
1. Close ESP Maker (and the game).
2. Replace the old "ESP Maker.exe" with the new one (or unzip the new folder anywhere and delete
   the old one). Don't delete %AppData%\ESP Maker.
3. Start the new version: your mods and checks are all there. The first start reads the game's
   item list again (a few seconds).
4. Press "Build & install" once for each of your mods to get the 1.1 fixes into the game.
Your installed .ba2 mods keep working in the game meanwhile - nothing is uninstalled.
